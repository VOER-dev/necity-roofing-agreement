4. Essential terms

  • Concealed or damaged decking, fascia, framing, and other unforeseen conditions require a written change order stating cost and time effects before extra work, except where immediate safety measures are necessary.
  • Contractor supplies included materials unless the signed scope expressly identifies owner-supplied materials.
  • Weather, inspections, concealed conditions and material delays may affect the schedule; changes will be communicated and documented as legally required.
  • Owner provides reasonable access; contractor is responsible for jobsite safety, agreed protection and cleanup.
  • Permits and inspections will be handled as specified above.
  • Warranty: workmanship term, covered work and exclusions must be specified in writing; manufacturer warranties are separate.
